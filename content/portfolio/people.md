## Growth

The part of the job I’m proudest of is the people, both while we work together and after they move on. Designers I hired early in their careers have gone on to become directors at Square and VPs at Capital One, among other roles.

## Retention

In 2021, half of Northwestern Mutual’s design practice had been there less than a year, with few mentors to learn from. We rebuilt career paths, and attrition fell from 36% that year to three people by July 2024. I was called a “talent magnet”: strong people I’ve worked with tend to follow me to new challenges.

## Structure

I like flat organizations when the work and culture support them, but I don’t see management as overhead. At Diligent I doubled the leadership team from five to ten. The org could take on more ambiguity and complexity, and designers had someone to work through hard problems instead of going it alone.
