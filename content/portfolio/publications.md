*Liftoff! Practical Design Leadership to Elevate Your Team, Your Organization, and You*, with Russ Unger (Rosenfeld Media, July 2020), foreword by Kim Goodwin. A practical guide for new and experienced design managers: hiring and scaling teams, developing careers, why diversity matters to the business, and establishing design’s place in the organization. It includes a step-by-step design charter workshop (Chapter 8) and ways to bring executives into common UX activities to build influence (Chapter 15).
