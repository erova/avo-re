situation: \>-
  "AI first" was a goal everyone was evaluated on and nobody had defined. Designers didn't know what it meant or how they'd be judged, while the industry was busy announcing design layoffs.
did: \>-
  In short: operationalized culture change. Addressed the ambiguity of “use AI” by defining what AI use looks like at every level, from junior to staff. I wrote a triad playbook with product and engineering that treats AI prototypes as hypotheses, not proof, and makes clear when design enters the work. I designed a readiness scorecard to better understand where we were as the current state and where we needed to improve, both as a design organization and broader company to fully be AI native. 
outcomes:
  - Designers understood what was expected and how they’d be evaluated for using AI
  - Leaders felt greater clarity when understanding where AI made tangible gains and where last-mile or final delivery challenges persisted 
  - Teams felt less ambiguity and anxiety with clear guidance and direction
